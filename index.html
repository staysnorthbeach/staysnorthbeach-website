GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How does direct booking work?

Select your preferred available dates and send a booking request. Mel then personally rechecks availability and guest details and confirms the final direct price. Your request is not an automatic booking.

Is the website availability calendar live?

The website reads booked and blocked dates from Airbnb, which remains our master calendar. Calendar feeds can take time to update, so we always complete a final Airbnb check before confirming your stay.

What does “save 8%” mean?

For the same dates, our direct accommodation rate is 8% cheaper than the comparable accommodation price advertised on Airbnb. The final direct price is provided with your booking response.

Are the two stays private from each other?

Yes. The Midnight Hideaway is a fully separate rear guesthouse with its own rear entrance and dedicated parking. The Front Residence has its own private entrance, driveway and parking. The main property gate is the only shared access point.

When will I hear back after an enquiry?

You will receive an immediate acknowledgement after submitting the form. Mel then reviews the dates and guest details and will be in contact very soon.

How are arrival and departure details provided?

Once a stay is confirmed, your arrival, access and departure details are provided directly so you know exactly what to expect before your stay.
