PRIVATE · DESIGN-LED · ATMOSPHERIC

The Midnight Hideaway

A distinctive approximately 90 m² private rear guesthouse designed for guests who value atmosphere, privacy and thoughtful detail. The open-plan kitchen and living area combines exposed brick, rich finishes and warm lighting, while two bedrooms and two bathrooms make longer stays feel easy.

The second bedroom can be arranged as two king singles or one super-king, giving couples, friends and families more flexibility. A private rear entrance and dedicated parking keep the stay completely independent, with only the main property gate shared.

RELAXED · RENOVATED · COASTAL

North Beach House

A beautifully renovated private coastal retreat for up to four guests, combining timeless character with modern comfort. Rich Japan Black Jarrah timber floors, light-filled interiors and a calm coastal palette create a home that feels polished without losing its relaxed North Beach character.

Two queen bedrooms, a new designer bathroom, fully equipped kitchen and generous lounge and dining spaces make it ideal for couples, families, friends, business travellers and longer stays. Full-width bi-fold doors connect the home to a private enclosed front courtyard, while the landscaped rear garden provides a second peaceful outdoor retreat.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know before you enquire.

How does direct booking work?

Select your preferred stay and dates, then submit the enquiry form. Mel will recheck the Airbnb calendar, review the guest details and confirm the final direct price before the booking is accepted.

Is the calendar live?

The website reads booked and blocked dates from Airbnb via calendar sync. Calendar feeds can occasionally take time to refresh, so we always make one final availability check before confirming your stay.

Is booking direct cheaper?

Yes. Our direct accommodation rate is 8% cheaper than the comparable accommodation price advertised on Airbnb for the same dates. Your final direct price is confirmed with your booking request.

Is my enquiry an automatic booking?

No. Submitting the form sends a booking request only. Your stay is confirmed after availability, guest details and the final price have been reviewed.

What happens after I submit?

You will immediately see a confirmation page. Once our email service is active, you will also receive an acknowledgement by email. Mel will then contact you with the next steps.

Are the two stays private?

Yes. Both stays have their own private accommodation areas and entrances. The Midnight Hideaway is at the rear of the property with dedicated parking, while North Beach House has its own entrance, driveway and parking.
